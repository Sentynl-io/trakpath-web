The Legacy Burden

01. Manual Inaccuracy

Paper logs and manual spreadsheet entries are highly susceptible to human error and intentional padding of bulk materials.

02. Information Silos

Documentation is often trapped in physical manifests or disconnected local databases, making real-time oversight impossible.

03. High CAPEX Bloat

Traditional tracking requires expensive RFID tags, specialized scanners, and heavy infrastructure that is difficult to scale.

04. Audit Liability

The "pencils-up" phenomenon: audits take days or weeks of forensic reconstruction, leaving firms vulnerable to regulatory fines.

05. Chain of Custody Blindspots

Unverified transit periods create "black boxes" where batch swapping and test-shopping occur without detection.
