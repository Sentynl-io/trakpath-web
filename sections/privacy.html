Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

01. Introduction

Welcome to TrakPath, a product of MiJory LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and web console. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

02. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

  • Location Data (GPS DNA Anchoring) We request access to track location-based information from your mobile device while you are using our App to provide our core "Hydration Engine" services. This is strictly utilized to anchor geographic provenance to supply chain scans and ensure jurisdictional compliance. You can change access permissions in your device's settings.
  • Photographic Data The App requires camera access to scan QR/Barcodes and to capture visual verification photos of supply chain assets. These images are stored securely as part of the immutable ledger.
  • Personal & Organizational Data Personally identifiable information (name, email, role) voluntarily given when registering an Organization. This is required for Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and audit accountability.

03. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide a secure, efficient, and customized experience. We use information collected to:

Provide core tracking functionality
Manage RBAC and user accounts
Generate compliance reports
Prevent fraudulent transfers

04. Information Sharing

We only share information with third parties that perform critical services for our infrastructure. TrakPath utilizes Amazon Web Services (AWS) for secure serverless hosting and database management, and Stripe for payment processing. We do not sell your personal or organizational data to third parties.

05. Retention & Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary. If you wish to delete your account or request that we delete data collected about you, please contact your Organization Administrator or email our support team. Please note that due to the nature of our immutable ledger technology, certain anonymized scan records may be retained indefinitely to preserve the structural integrity of previous supply chain audits.
