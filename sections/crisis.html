Market Intelligence

The Crisis of
Fragmented Systems.

Current supply chain and compliance platforms are leaving global leaders frustrated. The core market complaint isn’t a lack of tools—it’s a lack of truth, automation, and integration.

01

“It’s still not truly end-to-end.”

Platforms are often weak deeper in the network. Only 60% of respondents had comprehensive visibility of tier-1 suppliers, while deeper tier visibility declined for a second straight year.

Source: McKinsey & Company
02

“Integration is a nightmare.”

Data quality and availability are the most common challenges. Data silos make it hard to stitch together procurement, logistics, manufacturing, and planning data.

McKinsey World Economic Forum
03

“Too much manual work.”

Buyers still live in spreadsheets and manual evidence gathering. 88% of respondents still use manual processes often or sometimes despite paying for "automation."

Source: Wolters Kluwer
04

“Lagging behind regulatory churn.”

Rules change faster than systems. 85% say compliance requirements became more complex in 3 years, with 43% of CECOs seeing new rules as their #1 challenge.

PwC KPMG
05

“Weak interoperability.”

Independent schemes cause reporting duplication and audit fatigue. Harmonized assessments and open traceability standards are critical for resource efficiency.

WEF GS1 ITC
06

“Weaker ROI than promised.”

92% of supply chain leaders say tech investments have not fully delivered results. One-third lack quantified business cases for implementation.

PwC McKinsey
07

“Adoption is harder than vendors admit.”

Rollouts stall because users lack trust, skills, or time. People issues—skills shortages and change-management—are nearly as common as technical barriers. 90% of companies lack sufficient talent to meet digitization goals.

Source: McKinsey & Company

The Core Opportunity Gap.

Current solutions are fragmented, manual, and too reactive. The clear market gap is for a system that combines multi-tier visibility, regulatory change management, and interoperable data into one unified workflow.
