01

Real-Time Dynamic Data Reconstruction ("Hydration")

Most legacy systems treat a QR code or serialized identifier as a pointer to a fixed, historical record. Once data is written (e.g., lab results, location, or status), updating it often requires new entries, manual reconciliation, or even physical relabeling.

TrakPath's edge: The Hydration Engine continuously ingests data from multiple sources (LIMS, ERP, regulatory rules engines, field scans/GPS/photos) and reconstructs the ground-truth state live at scan time. This delivers the most current compliance view and timeline without any changes to the physical label.

Traditional enterprise ledgers are strong at recording immutable events but generally deliver static snapshots rather than instant reconstruction.

02

Proactive, Built-in Regulatory Guardrails

TrakPath embeds state-specific rules engines that automatically enforce compliance before any next step in the supply chain (e.g., transfer, processing, or release) can proceed.

TrakPath's edge: If a product fails current jurisdictional requirements, the system flags it immediately. When regulations change, the new rules apply instantly to all existing inventory — no manual reprogramming needed.

First-generation compliance software typically offers reactive reporting. They rarely provide automatic enforcement checkpoints before workflow advancement.

03

Infinite Updates Without Relabeling

TrakPath's edge: Producers can update lab results, destinations, batch status, or compliance data at any time. The physical QR code remains unchanged for the product's entire life, eliminating costly reprinting, design work, plate changes, and inventory waste.

Platform decoupling of the physical anchor from the live data layer makes updates truly frictionless compared to reconciliation efforts on other platforms.

The Lab Blind Spot

Labs face $300k to $2M in fines for data they can't verify. "Lab shopping" and sample tampering happen in the gap between the field and the lab door where LIMS doesn't reach.

2. Lab In-Take Scan

Lab tech scans the sample on arrival. They instantly see the original collection photo and GPS to confirm a match. No more "blind" trust.

3. LIMS Integration

The lab runs tests in their existing LIMS. TrakPath doesn't replace software; it wraps it in an external trust layer.
